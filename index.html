Narendra Modi

Article Talk

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Narendra Damodardas Modi (born 17 September 1950) is an Indian politician who has served as the prime minister of India since 26 May 2014. He was the chief minister of Gujarat from 2001 to 2014 and is the Member of Parliament from Varanasi. He is a member of the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP).

Modi is the longest-serving prime minister from outside the Indian National Congress and the first prime minister born after India's independence. He led the BJP to a single-party majority in the 2014 general election, was re-elected in 2019, and began a third consecutive term in June 2024 at the head of the National Democratic Alliance.

Born in Vadnagar, Gujarat, he became a full-time worker of the Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh in 1971 and later rose through the BJP's organisation. His time in office has drawn both strong public approval and sustained criticism, including debate over the 2002 Gujarat riots during his tenure as chief minister.
